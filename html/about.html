Smart technology is only useful when it becomes part of the architecture.

TIS Control was founded in 2012 with a focus on making intelligent technology accessible, adaptable and useful.

TIS East Africa brings that technology into a regional context. From our Nairobi base, we work with homeowners, developers, architects, consultants and technology partners across the region.

We design around the project first — then bring lighting, climate, curtains, security, entertainment, energy management and other systems together into one connected environment.
